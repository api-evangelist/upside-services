# Upside

> Upside (Upside Services, Inc., upside.com) is a retail technology platform that delivers personalized cash-back offers for gas, grocery, and restaurant purchases. Consumers earn cash back through the Upside app and partner apps; merchants pay only for measured incremental profit. Upside's enterprise partner API powers offer delivery inside partner applications (Fiserv, GasBuddy, Lyft, Uber, Marqeta, and others), but the company publishes no public developer portal, API documentation, or SDKs — API access is arranged through its partnerships team.
